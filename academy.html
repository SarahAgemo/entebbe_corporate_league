ECL Sports Academy

A continuous youth development programme in football and swimming. Not a side event, and not only for the children of ECL members.

A programme that runs all year

The ECL Sports Academy operates throughout the year, in school terms and school holidays alike.

Children train regularly in football and swimming, work on fitness and physical education, and develop character and life skills alongside their sport. They also compete: in the ECL Kids League for football, and in swimming competitions.

The Academy is connected to the Corporate League, and both are part of ECL. But the Academy has its own programme, its own coaches and its own calendar. It does not only happen on corporate match days.

What children do at the Academy

Four parts to every child's programme.

  • Football training

    Regular coached sessions in age groups, building skills, positioning and match understanding.

  • Swimming

    Coached swimming for water confidence, technique and competition.

  • Physical education

    Fitness, movement and conditioning suited to each age group.

  • Character development

    Discipline, teamwork, respect and the habits that carry beyond sport.

  • Green action

    Children take part in ECL's tree planting, learning conservation by doing it.

From first session to competition

How a child progresses through the Academy.

  1. Join the Academy

    Register your child and we confirm the training group, days and venue.

  2. Train regularly

    Coached football or swimming sessions continue through terms and holidays.

  3. Compete

    Children play in the ECL Kids League and take part in swimming competitions.

  4. Develop

    Coaches track progress as children move up through age groups.

For parents

What your child gets, and what you need to know.

  • Coached football and swimming, grouped by age
  • A programme that continues during school holidays, not only in term time
  • Real competition, not just practice
  • Coaches who work on conduct and teamwork as well as skill
  • A wider ECL community of families and organisations

Training days, times, venues and fees are confirmed when you enrol, so you always get the current schedule.
