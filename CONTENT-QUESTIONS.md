# Content questions for ECL

The site avoids inventing facts. Everything below is either missing or unverified. Answers can be dropped straight into the pages named.

## ECL overall
1. **Founding year** and whether the season is annual. (We only know the 8th edition was 2024.) → `about.html`
2. **Legal/organisational status** — association, company, CBO? Useful for partners. → `about.html`, `partners.html`
3. **Mission and vision** — the current wording on `about.html` is a draft written from public material. Please confirm or replace.
4. **Current numbers** — organisations, players, children, trees planted to date, clean-ups held. Impact numbers are the single strongest thing we can add for partners. → `about.html`, `partners.html`
5. **Venue name** — sources say Lake Victoria Primary School (the first brief said "Lake Victory").

## Corporate League
6. **Season dates** for the coming season, and the registration deadline. → `corporate-league.html`, `news.html`
7. **Season fee** — the site says UGX 2,000,000 per organisation. Kawowo reported UGX 1,000,000 per team for 2024. Which is current, and is it per organisation or per team?
8. **What the fee covers** — kit, refreshments, medical, awards?
9. **Squad size limits** per discipline, and whether netball, volleyball and tug of war have their own team rules.
10. **Awards** — are there trophies, fair-play or off-field awards? These make good gallery and news content.

## ECL Sports Academy
11. **Training days, times and venues** for football and swimming. The kids' football photos were taken at ESA Park, not the school playground — which venues should be listed?
12. **Age groups** — the exact brackets used for training and competition.
13. **Fees** — monthly, termly or per session, and what they include.
14. **Coaches** — how many, qualifications, safeguarding arrangements. Parents look for this.
15. **Who can join** — only children of ECL member organisations, or any child in Entebbe?
16. **Kit and equipment** — what parents provide.
17. **Safeguarding and medical cover** — worth a short paragraph on the Academy page.
